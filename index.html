Individual Initiative

Warehouse & Custody System Automation

THE CHALLENGE

Custody records were kept on paper and in scattered Excel files. Finding an employee's custody items or processing a return meant searching file by file.

Approach

  • Started by consolidating the records into one database with Excel Power Query, Power BI, and Access, searchable by employee name.
  • Then built a web application (Python, Flask, SQLite) to replace the manual Excel and Power Query workflow for tracking employee custody items (issue, return, addition).
  • Structured the system around two core functions: personal custody tracking and returns processing.

Impact

  • In use at SCZONE by the staff responsible for custody records and returns.
  • Replaced file-by-file searches with instant lookup by employee name.
  • Built individually, with no external vendor or system integrator.
Individual Initiative

Warehouse Regulations Framework

THE CHALLENGE

SCZONE had no formal warehouse regulation. Inventory and custody procedures relied on informal practice instead of a documented framework.

Approach

  • Benchmarked reference frameworks, including those of the Ministry of Communications, the Financial Regulatory Authority, West Delta Electricity, and the government's standard warehouse regulations.
  • Drafted the full Warehouse Regulation: 13 chapters plus appendices, based on Egyptian government references and international standards, including ISO 55000 and CIPS. Used AI tools to speed up research and drafting.
  • Now in legal review ahead of formal approval.

Status & Impact

  • Once approved, it will be SCZONE's first documented warehouse regulation.
  • Prepared individually, without external consultancy support.
  • Reflects the same regulatory drafting and benchmarking discipline that goes into any large-scale governance framework.
